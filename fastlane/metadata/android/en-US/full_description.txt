Cyclauncher is a fast, ergonomic Android launcher built entirely with Jetpack Compose, designed from the ground up for comfortable one-handed navigation and instant app access.

Key Features:
- Ergonomic One-Handed Control: Complete layout mirroring for Left-Handed and Right-Handed use, keeping all apps and controls within natural thumb reach.
- Multi-Modal App Search: Rapid app retrieval through an ergonomic Side Alphabet Grid, an alternative rectangular alphabet wheel with inertia physics, chromatic icon color filtering, and instant text search.
- Interactive Tag Folders: Group applications with 2x2 live icon previews, proximity-aware popup menus, 2D drag-to-reorder on grid and within folders, and home screen favorite pinning.
- Highlights Hub & Widgets: Dedicated second screen featuring customizable Android widgets in compact accordions (search, resize, reconfigure), daily update tracking, and an untagged apps drawer.
- Private Space & Work Profiles: Built-in support for multi-profile environments (Personal, Work, Private Space) with biometric lock/unlock and isolated app caching.
- Character & Symbol Mapping: Customize search buckets by binding emojis, Cyrillic, Arabic, and international alphabets directly to A-Z and #.
- Adaptive Theming & Hue Angle Shift: Dynamic wallpaper theming powered by an adaptive Hue Angle Shift algorithm, Echo Icon Theme presets, custom HSV picker, and drop-shadow tuning.
- Privacy-First & Battery-Efficient: 100% Jetpack Compose with zero telemetry, no background network calls, and an accessibility toggle to disable all animations.
