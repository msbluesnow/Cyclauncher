Cyclauncher is not just yet another bicycle. Built with Jetpack Compose, it is focused on speed, effortless app accessibility, and seamless one-handed usability. New features and mechanics are continuously designed not just to be unique, but to deliver a genuinely convenient and ergonomic daily user experience.

Key Features:
- Tag Folder System: On-screen interactive tag folders with live icon previews and quick-access popups.
- Versatile Search Modes: Rectangular alphabet wheel, ergonomic side alphabet index strip, and instant text filtering.
- Ergonomic one-handed optimization with full Left/Right hand layout support.
- Dynamic accent colors, customizable main text color, and adaptive shadow inversion.
- Dynamic favorites with drag-and-drop reordering and launch history tracking.
- Fast, minimalist, and battery-efficient.
