First stable release of Cyclauncher: fast, ergonomic, and one-handed.
- Multi-modal app search: side alphabet grid, inertia wheel, color, and text.
- Interactive tag folders with 2D reordering and home pinning.
- Highlights hub with customizable widgets and daily tracking.
- Multi-profile support: biometric Private Space and Work profiles.
- Full backup and restore for settings, widgets, and layout.
- Native localization in 7 languages.
- R8 minification and Baseline Profiles (~2.2MB APK).
