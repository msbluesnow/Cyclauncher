Release Candidate 2 focusing on icon caching performance and localization.
- Enabled Coil 3 memory caching for app icons via AppIconKeyer and disk data source.
- Eliminated launcher resume freezes with fast-path icon resolving and async checks.
- Cached scaled icon shape masks to eliminate redundant allocations and transforms.
- Added full bilingual localization support with in-app language switching (EN/RU).
- Added marquee text scrolling toggle with touch-to-restart interaction.
